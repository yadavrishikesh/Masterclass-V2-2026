# Masterclass-V2-2026

## Summer School on Statistical Data Analytics & Deep Learning (Version 2)
For version~1, you may look at the (https://github.com/yadavrishikesh/Masterclass-IITMandi)

**School of Mathematical & Statistical Sciences (SMSS), IIT Mandi**  
**Date:** June 2–6, 2026  
**Mode:** Online

This repository contains the lecture slides, notebooks, assignments, exercises, datasets, and project materials used during the **Summer School on Statistical Data Analytics & Deep Learning – Version 2** conducted by the School of Mathematical & Statistical Sciences (SMSS), IIT Mandi.

The masterclass was designed to provide participants with a comprehensive introduction to modern Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Data Analytics, progressing from foundational machine learning concepts to advanced transformer architectures and real-world AI applications.

- **Dr. Rishikesh Yadav**, IIT Mandi
- **Prof. Sarita Azad**, IIT Mandi
- **Vedant Vibhor** (PhD Student at IIT Mandi, served as Teaching Assistant)
- **Gaurav** (MTech. Student at IIT Mandi, served as Teaching Assistant)

---

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this masterclass, participants would be able to:

- Perform data preprocessing and exploratory data analysis.
- Build machine learning and deep learning models using Python.
- Develop Neural Networks, CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs, and GRUs.
- Apply optimization and regularization techniques.
- Understand attention mechanisms and transformer architectures.
- Fine-tune pre-trained deep learning models.
- Design end-to-end AI workflows for structured, image, and sequential data.
